@topic eng-reported | Direct and reported speech | SS2

Q: Choose the correct reported form: He said, "I am tired."
A: He said that he was tired.
W: He said that I am tired.
W: He said that he is being tired.
W: He said that he had been tiring.
E: In reported speech, "I" becomes "he" and "am" moves back to "was".

Q: Choose the correct reported form: She said, "I will call you tomorrow."
A: She said that she would call me the next day.
W: She said that she will call me tomorrow.
W: She said that I would call her tomorrow.
W: She said that she called me the next day.
E: "Will" becomes "would" and "tomorrow" becomes "the next day".

Q: Choose the correct reported form: The teacher asked, "Have you finished?"
A: The teacher asked if we had finished.
W: The teacher asked have we finished.
W: The teacher asked if we have finished.
W: The teacher asked that had we finished.
E: Yes/no questions are reported with "if" or "whether", and "have finished" becomes "had finished".

Q: Choose the correct reported form: Mother said to me, "Close the door."
A: Mother told me to close the door.
W: Mother told me that close the door.
W: Mother said me to close the door.
W: Mother told me I closed the door.
E: Commands are reported with "told + person + to + verb".

Q: Choose the correct reported form: Musa said, "I bought this phone yesterday."
A: Musa said that he had bought that phone the day before.
W: Musa said that he bought this phone yesterday.
W: Musa said that I had bought that phone the day before.
W: Musa said that he has bought this phone yesterday.
E: The simple past moves back to the past perfect, "this" becomes "that" and "yesterday" becomes "the day before".

Q: Choose the correct reported form: Bisi asked, "Where do you live?"
A: Bisi asked where I lived.
W: Bisi asked where do I live.
W: Bisi asked where did I live.
W: Bisi asked that where I lived.
E: In reported questions the word order becomes a statement, and the tense moves back.
